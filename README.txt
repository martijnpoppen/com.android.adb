Control an Android phone or tablet from Homey: turn the screen on and off, set the brightness and media volume, see the battery level, and open apps or web pages from a Flow.

From a Flow you can also switch Bluetooth on or off, set Do Not Disturb and auto-rotate, show a notification, take a screenshot, tap or swipe on the screen, close an app or restart the device. Music playing on the device shows up in Homey with play, pause, next and previous. Changes to the screen, the app on screen, charging, battery, Bluetooth and the Wi-Fi network can start your Flows.

The app talks to the device directly over your local network using the Android Debug Bridge (ADB) protocol. Nothing needs to be installed on the device and no computer is needed.

On Android 11 and newer, turn on Wireless debugging in the developer options and pair with the pairing code shown on the device. The pairing screen in Homey has step-by-step instructions per brand. Older devices can use classic network ADB on port 5555.

Use the device as a wall display: wake the screen when someone enters the room, dim it in the evening, open your dashboard, and turn it off at night.
